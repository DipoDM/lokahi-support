FAQs

How do I sign in?

Use Apple, Google, or Email (magic link / one-time code). If you can’t access your email, contact us and we’ll help.

I didn’t receive a verification email.

Check spam/junk. Then tap “Open Mail App” in Ohana. Still stuck? Email us at ohana.support@gmail.com.

How do I report an issue with an event or host?

Email details (event name, date, screenshots if possible) to ohana.support@gmail.com.
